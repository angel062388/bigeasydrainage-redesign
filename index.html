Signs to watch for

Is your yard telling you it can't drain?

Spotting drainage trouble early protects your lawn, your plants and your foundation. These are the signs we hear about most.

Puddles that linger

Water that sits for days after rain and soil that stays soft means water isn't moving through your yard.

Water at the foundation

Low spots and poor grading can send water toward your home instead of away from it.

Drains that back up

Leaves and debris block grates, and existing drains that can't keep up overflow in heavy rain.

Stressed grass and plants

Waterlogged ground suffocates roots, leaving yellow leaves, bare patches and wilting grass.

How it works

Water in. Water out.

A French drain is a gravel trench with a perforated pipe inside. Rain soaks down through the gravel, enters the pipe and is carried away from your house and lawn.

  1. 1Rain lands on the lawn and soaks into the gravel.
  2. 2Water seeps down through the gravel into the perforated pipe.
  3. 3The pipe carries it to a safe outlet, away from the foundation.

Why it matters here

New Orleans yards have nowhere to send the water

Drainage here is a different job than in most of the country. That's why a system built for local conditions matters.

  • Much of the city sits below sea level and is surrounded by water, so rain has little room to run off.
  • Sitting between the Mississippi River and Lake Pontchartrain, the area is prone to flooding, and even a small amount of rain can overwhelm drainage.
  • Heavy rainfall and hurricanes often lead to flooding that damages homes and yards.

FAQs

Drainage questions, answered

Still have a question? Call (504) 285-9658 or send a message through our contact page.

How can I tell if I have drainage problems on my property?

Look for signs like standing water, soggy patches, erosion, or water collecting around the foundation. These can all point to poor drainage.

What are the common causes of poor landscape drainage?

Poor grading, compacted soil, blocked or inadequate drainage systems, and poor planning are some of the most common causes.

How do I know which drainage solution is right for my property?

We assess your property's slope and drainage issues, then recommend the solution that suits it best.

How long does a typical drainage project take?

It depends on the problem and the solution. Simple fixes might take a day, while bigger projects can take several days.

Will drainage change how my landscape looks?

We design drainage to blend into your landscape, so your yard keeps its look.
